# Loans, Debts and Cards

How LekhaMint treats money that is owed, in the web app and the Android app.

## Lent and borrowed (people)

Open **Lent & borrowed** (web) or **More → Lent & borrowed** (Android) and add an entry.

- **Date:** *Lent on* / *Borrowed on* is the real date, so you can enter loans from months or years ago. It sets the start of the statement and the dating of the money movement.
- **Money moved or not:** tick *The money left (or came into) one of my accounts* when an account balance should change. Leave it unticked for money that moved before you started using LekhaMint; only the debt is recorded.
- **Repayments:** *Record repayment* takes a date, so late entries land on the day it really happened. Part payments keep the rest outstanding; interest or extra goes in its own field and is income or cost, not repayment.
- **Overpaid:** if someone repays more than they owe, the debt shows as overpaid and flips to the other side in net worth.
- **Settled:** *Mark settled* writes off what is left. No money moves.
- Lent and borrowed money is never counted as income or spending.

## Credit cards

A card balance is shown as **owed**. Paying the card bill is a *transfer* from your bank account to the card.

- **Paid too much?** The card then shows *credit* instead of *owed* and the credit is added to your available limit. It counts in your favour in net worth.
- **Starting in credit:** when adding the card, enter the opening amount as a negative number, such as `-500`.
- **Reconcile:** enter what your statement says you owe; LekhaMint records the difference as an adjustment.

## Bank loans and deposits

**Loans & EMIs** and **Deposits** keep their own schedules. Their linked accounts are managed from those pages.
